Animation brings models made to animate to life: a character walking, a door swinging, a flag in the wind.

1. [[Animation Clips]]: clips as assets, from glTF and FBX files, and how each moves
2. [[Animator]]: plays one clip
3. [[Motion Matching]]: a character that walks, runs, stops and turns as the game moves it, from its clips alone
4. [[Motion Sets]]: the states a character gets about in and the actions it plays
5. [[Actions]]: clips played once over a state, as jumping, throwing or dying, with marks scripts hear
6. [[Bone Attachments]]: an Obj kept on a bone, as eyes on a head or a gun in a hand
7. [[First-Person View]]: a camera in a character's own head
8. [[Bones in Scripts]]: reading a model's bones, and bending them after animation
