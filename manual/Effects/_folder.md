Effects are the moving touches that make a place feel alive: sparks off a grinder, smoke from a chimney, dust in a sunbeam, spray off the sea.

1. [[Particle Systems]]: sparks, smoke, spray and dust sent out from an Obj
2. [[Smoke]]: clouds that fill a room and pour out of its doors, as smoke grenades do
3. [[Mist]]: a trail of fog left behind a moving Obj, as a wizard's dash leaves
4. [[Craters]]: shell holes blasted into the ground
