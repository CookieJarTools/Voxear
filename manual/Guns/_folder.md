Guns are held, aimed, fired and reloaded in your character's own hands. Everything a gun does is worked out as it happens, from what the gun is: its kind, its weight, where its sights and grip are, and how its parts move. A Kar98k's bolt is lifted, pulled and pushed home because that is how its bolt moves, not because someone animated it. An action can also be based on a clip of your own, which moves the body and carries the gun while the hands stay on it. And a gun that comes with its own animation, from an animation pack, can be held and handled by its clips instead.

Guns is a module: switch it on in **Project Settings** first (see [[Modules]]). It needs a character with a [[First-Person View]].

1. [[Setting Up a Gun]]: the Gun component, its kind, sockets and parts
2. [[Holding and Aiming]]: the Gun Holder, the hip, the sights, and the sway, bob and breathing
3. [[Firing and Recoil]]: shots, their spread, and how the view climbs and comes back
4. [[Reloads and Checks]]: reloading, working the bolt, and looking at how much is left
5. [[Guns over the Network]]: who decides what, in networked play
6. [[Gun Handling]]: how a gun is held, and how its actions play
7. [[Guns from Animation Packs]]: guns that come with clips of their own
