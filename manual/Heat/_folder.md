Every place in a level has a temperature: warm in the afternoon and cold before dawn, mild in a cave, hot beside lava and cold on ice. Food left somewhere hot enough cooks, and left in the snow it freezes. Characters feel the cold and the heat, and warm clothes keep the cold out.

1. [[Temperature]]: the level's climate and clock, and the hot and cold things that change a place's temperature
2. [[Heat Bodies]]: food that cooks and freezes, characters that feel the cold, and how scripts hear of both

Every part of it is under **Add Component ▸ Heat**.
