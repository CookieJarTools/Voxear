A **Recipe** makes an item from others. Choose **New Data Asset…** and pick **Items ▸ Recipe**.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | **Shaped**: the items must lie as the grid shows them. **Shapeless**: the same items in any places |
| The grid | Three rows of three places, each an item or none |
| Result | The item it makes |
| Count | How many of it |
| Station | Where it is made, a [[Tool Kinds and Stations#Stations|station]]; none for anywhere |
| Time | How long it takes, for a station that takes its time |

## Where a shaped recipe's items may lie

A shaped recipe is matched by its shape, not by where in the grid it sits. Two sticks one above the other match in the left column, the middle or the right, in the top two rows or the bottom two, and in a larger grid anywhere at all. A recipe also matches mirrored left to right, so an axe's head may point either way.

What does not match is another arrangement: the sticks side by side, a gap between them, or anything else in the grid besides them.

A recipe two places wide fits a grid two by two, so it can be made by hand; one three wide needs a grid of three.

## Recipes in a build

Every recipe ships, named by anything or not, since a game finds them all at once rather than one by a field. Each brings the items it names.
