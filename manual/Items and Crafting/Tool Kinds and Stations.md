## Tool kinds

A **Tool Kind** is a kind of tool: a pickaxe, an axe, a shovel, or whatever your game has. It is nothing but its name. Choose **New Data Asset…** and pick **Items ▸ Tool Kind**, and name the file for the kind.

A block's **Tool** names the kind that breaks it fast, and an item's **Tool** the kind it is. So a game adds a kind of tool, a drill or a wand, by making a data asset, with no code.

Tiers are numbers on the item and the block: a wooden pickaxe of tier 1, a stone one of tier 2, and iron ore that wants tier 2 to drop anything.

## Stations

A **Station** is a place things are made at: a workbench, a furnace, an anvil. It too is nothing but its name: **New Data Asset… ▸ Items ▸ Station**.

A [[Recipes|recipe]] with a Station is made only there. What a station is in the world is yours: a block the player uses, an Obj they walk up to. Whatever opens the crafting says which station it is at.
