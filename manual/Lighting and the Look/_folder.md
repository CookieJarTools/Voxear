How a level looks comes from four things:

1. [[Lights]]: the sun, lamps and torches.
2. [[The Sky]]: its colours, and the soft light it spreads over everything.
3. [[Cameras]]: what the player sees through, and how bright the picture is.
4. [[Post-Processing]]: the finishing touches on the picture, such as glow, mist and colour.
