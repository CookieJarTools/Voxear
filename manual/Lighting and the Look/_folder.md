How a level looks comes from these:

1. [[Lights]]: the sun, lamps and torches.
2. [[The Sky]]: its colours, its clouds, and the soft light it spreads over everything.
3. [[Weather]]: cloud, fog, wind, rain and snow over the level's lighting, which scripts can turn.
4. [[Storms]]: weather with a place, that drifts over the level, rains where it is mild and snows where it is cold.
5. [[Bounced Light]]: light reflecting from one surface onto the next.
6. [[Cameras]]: what the player sees through, and how bright the picture is.
7. [[Post-Processing]]: the finishing touches on the picture, such as glow, mist and colour.

[[Lighting Settings]] keep a level's sky, sun, post-processing and weather together in one file, to light any scene with or to change to as the game runs.

[[Look Packs]] are a few of those settings laid over any level's lighting, which whoever plays the game picks: Voxear's own, yours, and any a player adds.

[[Texture Packs]] are the pictures blocks and materials are drawn with, which whoever plays the game picks too: yours, any a player adds, and a grain Voxear makes of each block's colour.
