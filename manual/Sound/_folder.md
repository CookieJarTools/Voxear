Voxear plays the sound files in your project: effects such as footsteps and doors, ambience such as wind and rain, and music.

1. [[Sounds and Audio Sources]]: bringing sound files in, and playing them from Objs
2. [[Playing Sounds from Scripts]]: sounds played once, Audio Sources, and the volumes a settings menu changes

> [!NOTE]
> Sound does not echo off walls or sound muffled behind them yet, and a passing car does not change pitch as it goes by. Those come in a later version of Voxear.
