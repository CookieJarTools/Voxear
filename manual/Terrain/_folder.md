Voxear's terrain is ground from heights: hills, fields and battlefields, drawn finely near the camera and more coarsely far off, with physics standing things on it and scripts asking about it.

Terrain is a module: switch it on in **Project Settings** first (see [[Modules]]).

1. [[Terrains]]: making a terrain, its tools, its size and resolution, how it is drawn, and asking about its ground from scripts
2. [[Layers]]: what the ground is made of, stamps, noise, roads and masks, each changed or moved at any time
3. [[Sculpting]]: brushes that raise, lower, smooth and flatten the ground in the Scene view

Terrain is under **Add Component ▸ Terrain**.
