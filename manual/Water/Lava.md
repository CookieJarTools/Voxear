Set a [[Pools and Dry Areas|Pool's]] or a [[Streams|Stream's]] **Liquid** to **Lava**, and it fills with molten rock instead. Lava glows by its temperature, as hot rock does: orange-yellow where it is freshest, dull red as it cools, then black. It has no waves, and nothing sees through it.

## Lava flows

A Stream of lava runs a dozen times slower than water down the same slope, as thick as lava is. Each part of it knows how long it has taken to come from where it set out, and cools as it goes:

- **At its source**, it is molten and bright.
- **As it cools**, a skin forms on it, in plates that ride along with the flow and crowd together where it slows. The molten lava under them glows through the cracks between them, and the cracks narrow as it cools.
- **While the skin is soft**, it folds into ropes across the flow, bowed downstream where the middle runs faster than the edges: pahoehoe, as on Hawaii's lava fields.
- **Its edges** crust over first, into levees either side of the molten middle.
- **Far from its source**, it has set into dark, glassy rock, with only a faint glow in its deepest cracks.

**Cooling** says how many seconds it runs before it crusts over, and so how far down the flow the crust begins. **Glow** is how bright it shines.

Lava that runs into the sea crusts over at once.

## Lava lakes

A Pool of lava is a lava lake: plates of crust drifting slowly across it, molten lava glowing in the cracks between them, its surface turning over in places. Raised above the ground, its sides are rock with glowing seams, hotter deeper down. Its surface turns over every quarter of a minute or so, so its **Cooling** sets how much of it has crusted: a few seconds for a lake that is nearly solid, a minute for one that is mostly molten.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Cooling | How many seconds it takes to crust over |
| Glow | How brightly it glows |

Lava's glow lights only itself. To light what is round it, add a few orange point [[Lights]] along it.

The built-in **Lava** shader (see [[Custom Shaders]]) draws lava on a mesh of your own instead, for a pool of any shape or a lava fall.
