Voxear's water is built in: a sea with waves as tall as Sea of Thieves', lakes and ponds at any height, holes the water stays out of, rivers that run where you draw them, flows of lava on the same system, and Objs that float on all of it.

1. [[The Ocean]]: the sea, its weather, its foam, and the world under it
2. [[Pools and Dry Areas]]: water held in a box at any height, and places water stays out of, such as a boat's hull
3. [[Streams]]: rivers and brooks running along a Path you draw in the Scene view
4. [[Lava]]: Pools and Streams of lava that glow, crust over and crack as they cool
5. [[Floating Objects]]: Buoyancy, and asking about the water from scripts

Every part of it is under **Add Component ▸ Water**.
