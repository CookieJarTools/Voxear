A world of blocks without end is generated first and built on after: the generator gives land you are happy with, and you set buildings on it, level their plots, and let what stands on the ground come and go with it as the world streams round the player.

Everything here works on the ground the generator makes without storing blocks. Move or delete what you placed and the ground comes back as it was.

1. [[World Layers]]: what changes the generator's ground without storing blocks, in what order, and how a change is made again
2. [[Ground Fit]]: a building that levels the ground it stands on, with a slope out to the ground round it
3. [[Streams With Ground]]: houses, props and mobs that go when their ground streams out, and come back with it

World building is under **Add Component ▸ Terrain**.
