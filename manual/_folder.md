This manual shows you how to make games with Voxear. It comes with the engine, so it always matches the version you have open.

## New to Voxear?

Start with [[Getting Started]]. It takes you from installing Voxear to a game you can hand to someone else:

1. [[Installing Voxear]]
2. [[Your First Project]]
3. [[A Tour of the Editor]]
4. [[Playing Your Game]]
5. [[Making a Build]]

Then read [[Objs and Components]] and [[Your First Script]].

## What the manual covers

- **[[Getting Started]]**: installing, the editor, playing and building.
- **[[Objs and Levels]]**: the pieces every game is made of, and how they load.
- **[[Scripting]]**: writing your game's code in C#, and components and systems for when there are thousands of something.
- **[[Models and Materials]]**: bringing in 3D models, and giving surfaces their colour.
- **[[Lighting and the Look]]**: lights, the sky, and the finishing touches on the picture.
- **[[Game UI]]**: menus and on-screen displays.
- **[[Sound]]**: sound effects, ambience and music, in 2D and placed in the world.
- **[[Physics]]**: things that fall, tumble and bump into each other, and characters that walk.
- **[[Water]]**: the sea, lakes, rivers and lava, and things that float on them.
- **[[Languages]]**: your game's text in each language you translate it into.
- **[[Content and Builds]]**: what goes into a build, and how the game loads things and lets them go.
- **[[Networking]]**: hosting and joining games, players' input over the network, and dedicated servers.
- **[[Guns]]**: guns held, aimed, fired and reloaded in your character's own hands, with no animation clips.
- **[[Finding Problems]]**: the Console, the Profiler, and the other tools for finding out why.
- **[[VoxDocs]]**: writing your project's own pages, guides that do the work for the reader, and linking code to pages.
- **[[Packages]]**: sharing scripts, tools and assets between projects, from Git, folders and registries.

## Reading the manual

- **Find a page**, at the top of the tree, searches the manual along with your own pages.
- **Ctrl+K**, anywhere in the editor, opens any page by its title.
- Your own pages can link to the manual's, as `[[manual:Prefabs]]`.
- The **Code Reference**, under the manual in the tree, has a page for every type in Voxear's C# API, with what each part of it does.

You cannot change the manual's pages here, since a new version of Voxear replaces them. Write your own notes in your project's pages instead.
