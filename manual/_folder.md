This manual shows you how to make games with Voxear. It comes with the engine, so it always matches the version you have open.

## New to Voxear?

Start with [[Getting Started]]. It takes you from installing Voxear to a game you can hand to someone else:

1. [[Installing Voxear]]
2. [[Your First Project]]
3. [[A Tour of the Editor]]
4. [[Playing Your Game]]
5. [[Making a Build]]

Then read [[Objs and Components]] and [[Your First Script]].

## What is here

- **[[Getting Started]]**: installing, the editor, playing and building.
- **[[Objs and Levels]]**: the pieces every game is made of, and how they load.
- **[[Scripting]]**: writing your game's code in C#.
- **[[VoxDocs]]**: writing your project's own pages, guides that do the work for the reader, and linking code to pages.

## Reading the manual

- **Find a page**, at the top of the tree, searches the manual along with your own pages.
- **Ctrl+K**, anywhere in the editor, opens any page by its title.
- Your own pages can link to the manual's, as `[[manual:Prefabs]]`.
- The **Code Reference**, under the manual in the tree, has a page for every type in Voxear's C# API, with what each part of it does.

You cannot change the manual's pages here, since a new version of Voxear replaces them. Write your own notes in your project's pages instead.
